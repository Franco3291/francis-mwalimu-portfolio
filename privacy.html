Privacy

This portfolio does not publish visitor information.

Information handling

Contact and feedback messages are only sent to the configured owner email or HTTPS form provider. No visitor details are displayed publicly.

Analytics

Analytics are optional and should be configured with privacy-respecting, aggregate reporting only. Do not add credentials or private keys to frontend files.

Third-party services

External links and fonts are subject to their providers' privacy policies. Replace placeholder services and URLs before deployment.
